### About

Flutter is where I spend most of my free time — turning ideas into small, polished tools that other developers can just drop in and use.

I care about clean APIs, real documentation, and craftsmanship in the details most people skip. Building in the open, one focused package at a time.

Solo dev, open-source by choice, coffee-powered by necessity.
